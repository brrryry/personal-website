While doing this, I used a few different websites to help me get better at the game. One that stood out to me was the 
<a href="https://osu-skillset-analyzer.vercel.app/" target="_blank">osu! skillset analyzer</a> made by user <a href="https://osu.ppy.sh/users/23777414" target="_blank">Kumokoni</a>.
This website scans your top 200 plays, pinned scores, recent plays, and your favorite maps. Then, it calculates a 12-axis user skill portfolio.

This website is fantastic, and I have no complaints. It has a very slick UI, and the portfolio is explain EXTREMELY well.
It inspired me to look a little bit deeper. What if we could try to recommend maps, but based on axis-free embeddings? \
\
When I talk about "axis-free", I'm referring the removal of the 12-axis classification.
After scrolling through quite a few forums, I found that different people have different ideas of what defines an "axis" of skill. Some examples may include:

<BlogList>
<li>Aim Control</li>
<li>Stamina</li>
<li>Reading Slow/Fast Approach Rates</li>
<li>Finger Control</li>
<li>Complex Pattern Reading</li>
<li>Precision</li>
<li>The list goes on...</li>
</BlogList>

So, what if we didn't use any axes to remove disparity? That's what this project aims to do.
